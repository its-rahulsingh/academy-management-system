How to feed Gemini, piece by piece

Gemini’s video model makes one clip of about 8 seconds per request and remembers nothing between clips. So every prompt here is complete on its own, and the people are described the same way every time. Do one card per message.

Cast first. Generate each character’s reference photo with Gemini’s image tool (Cast section). Pick the best one of each and save it. These photos keep faces the same across scenes.
Make each shot’s first frame. For every shot, send the Frame prompt with that character’s reference photo attached. Keep the image that matches best.
Animate it. Start a video request, attach that first-frame image, and send the shot’s Video prompt. It adds movement, voice and sound. Generate 2–3 takes and keep the best.
Tick it off here. Video generations are limited per day on Gemini plans, so spread the shoot over a few days. Ticks are saved in this browser.
Edit. Drop the clips and the three Ojas screen inserts into CapCut or Canva in the order on the timeline, then add music, subtitles and the end card (Edit section).

Do not ask Gemini to draw the Ojas app. AI video garbles screens and text. Every prompt keeps phone and laptop screens facing away. The real app comes from your own product tour video, cut in between scenes.

Cast

Generate these as still photos first. Paste the description exactly; the same words appear inside every shot prompt, which is what keeps people consistent.

Shots

Each AI shot has two prompts: the Frame (a still image to start from) and the Video (motion, voice, sound). Attach the cast photo when making the frame, and the frame when making the video.

Edit and finish

Cut in CapCut or Canva

  • Lay out clips in timeline order. Trim each AI clip to the line plus half a second.
  • Inserts A–B: record your product tour and cut the timestamps listed on each insert card.
  • Add subtitles for every line (most people watch muted), in English or Hindi.
  • Music: a light, hopeful track from CapCut’s commercial-use library or the YouTube Audio Library, at about 15% volume under speech.
  • Export 16:9 for YouTube and WhatsApp, and 9:16 for Reels.

If something comes out wrong

  • Face changed between shots: remake that shot’s frame with the cast photo attached, then animate again.
  • “Ojas” said wrongly: keep the clip, mute it, and record that line yourself or with a voice tool over the top.
  • Hindi sounds off: regenerate once; if it is still off, use the English line given under each Hindi line.
  • Garbled text or screens appear: add “no visible text anywhere” and regenerate.

Before you post

  • Add a small “Characters created with AI” line in the end card or caption. It keeps the ad honest with viewers.
  • Make sure no character looks like a real celebrity or a real person you know.
  • Claims must match the product: free up to 25 students, paid plans from ₹199.
